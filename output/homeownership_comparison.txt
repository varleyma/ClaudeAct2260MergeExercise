HOMEOWNERSHIP RATE COMPARISON
Reports panel (true homeownership) vs. karibe name-search (found)
=============================================================

A) REPORTS-BASED HOMEOWNERSHIP  (person level, N=4726 report filers)
   Panel is a MIXED population: BOTH Act 22 and Act 60 decree holders.
   Every panel row matched the ownership crosswalk (100%).
   HOMEOWNER = owns a primary home (explicit 'Own' OR primary value>0):
      ever 'Own' response:             57.9%
      OWNS A HOME (Own OR value>0):     58.3%
   Context only, NOT homeownership:
      owns real estate ASSETS (re>0):  49.2%  (may be investment/land, not a home)

B) KARIBE NAME-SEARCH  (share of decree holders found w/ >=1 property)
   Act 22 names:   50.3%   (1574 of 3132)
   Act 60 names:   54.0%   (1416 of 2620)
   ALL names (22+60):  52.0%   (2990 of 5752)

INTERPRETATION
Like-for-like (both acts): reports say ~58% of filers OWN A HOME,
but the name search turned up property for only 52% of all decree
names. The search rate is inflated by same-name false positives
(searching 'Gary Smith' returns every Gary Smith), so it is an UPPER
bound - yet it still sits below the true homeownership rate.

=> Implied recall CEILING = kar_all / reports_home =  89%.
   The search missed at least ~11% of home-owning decree
   holders (more once false positives are netted out).

   (Note: karibe also captures NON-home property, e.g. investment
    lots/timeshares, which pushes its rate UP -- so the true miss
    rate for homeowners is larger than the gap above suggests.)

CAVEATS
- Reports are redacted (no names): aggregate-rate comparison only.
- Populations differ slightly: report FILERS vs. all named decree holders.
- Homeownership = self-reported primary home; karibe = ANY registry
  property (primary home, investment, timeshare).
