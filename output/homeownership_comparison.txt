HOMEOWNERSHIP RATE COMPARISON
Reports panel (true ownership) vs. karibe name-search (found)
=============================================================

A) REPORTS-BASED  (person level, N=4726 report filers)
   Panel is a MIXED population: BOTH Act 22 and Act 60 decree holders.
   Every panel row matched the ownership crosswalk (100%).
   Owns primary home (ever 'Own'):     57.9%
   Owns real estate assets (re>0):     49.2%
   Owns ANY property (home OR re>0):    62.3%

B) KARIBE NAME-SEARCH  (share of decree holders found w/ >=1 property)
   Act 22 names:   50.3%   (1574 of 3132)
   Act 60 names:   54.0%   (1416 of 2620)
   ALL names (22+60):  52.0%   (2990 of 5752)

INTERPRETATION
Like-for-like (both acts): reports say ~62% of filers own PR
property, but the name search turned up property for only 52% of
all decree names. Because the search rate is inflated by same-name
false positives (searching 'Gary Smith' returns every Gary Smith),
it is an UPPER bound - yet it still sits below the true ownership rate.

=> Implied recall CEILING = kar_all / reports_any =  83%.
   The search missed at least ~17% of property-owning
   decree holders (more once false positives are netted out).

CAVEATS
- Reports are redacted (no names): aggregate-rate comparison only.
- Populations differ slightly: report FILERS vs. all named decree holders.
- Reports = self-reported primary home + RE assets; karibe = any
  registry property (primary, investment, timeshare).
