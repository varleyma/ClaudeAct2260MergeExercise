## Results
- Transition: P(gains t+1 | gains t) = 72.6% vs 41.9% if no gains in
  t (1.7x; 3,676 consecutive pairs).
- Within person (observed >=3 years, ever-gains; n=827): median 2/3
  of observed years have gains; 59.6% have gains in the majority of
  years; 19.7% in ALL years. Histogram mass at the right.
- Dollars: 62.6% of exempt-gains dollars from persons with gains in
  >=2 distinct years (40.9% for 3+). The 37.4% single-gains-year
  share is an UPPER bound on lumpy exits: persons observed once
  (3,071 of 4,726) mechanically cannot show recurrence.
