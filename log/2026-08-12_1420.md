## Notes / caveats
- Experian zip_cd is USPS zip; treatment is on ZCTA polygons. 237 USPS zips vs
  132 ZCTAs — non-ZCTA zips are mostly PO-box zips; they appear in the migration
  panel but can't receive polygon-based treatment (they're untreated by construction —
  consider dropping non-ZCTA zips in analysis, or crosswalking).
- Migration = zip change between consecutive archive years; within-zip moves invisible;
  consumers must have a credit file (adults, credit-active — coverage caveat for PR).
- Header year is archive vintage (point-in-time address snapshot each year).

## Addendum (15:00) — Zip-level outmigration DiD: NULL across the board

`code/stata/zip_migration_did.do` (ran Stata 18 MP batch): lpdid mirroring
design2 (annual, pre 4 / post 3), zip×year panel restricted to the 132 true
ZCTAs (2,367 cells; PO-box zips dropped). Outcomes ×100 (pp). 13 specs:
off-PR / any-move / within-PR × base / zip3-FE / municipio-FE; dose 5+ and
1-4 vs never-treated; n_at_risk≥20 robustness.

Municipio FE: user asked if feasible — YES. Built area-based dominant-municipio
crosswalk (`build_zcta_muni_crosswalk.py` → `pr_zcta_muni_crosswalk.csv`):
median dominant share 99.4%, 128/132 ZCTAs ≥80% in one municipio. FE variant
reported; results invariant to FE choice.

RESULTS: everything null, clean flat pre-trends.
- Off-island rate: Post -0.16pp (se 0.28) base; -0.11 muni-FE; dose5+ +0.19 (se 0.39).
- Any-move rate: Post +0.50pp (se 0.90) base; dose5+ +0.50 (se 1.42); min20 -0.47 (se 0.44).
- Event studies: no dynamics, coefficients oscillate around 0.
Power: base off-PR rate ~2.7%/yr → 95% CI rules out increases >~0.4pp (~15% of
base) and decreases >~0.7pp. So: no evidence investor arrivals displaced
existing credit-active residents at the zip level (differential vs never-treated
zips), but modest sensitivity. Contrast with strong price effects (design2
+7.55) — prices capitalized, no detectable differential displacement in this sample.
Files: output/experian/zip_migration_coefs.dta/.csv, zip_migration_results.log.
