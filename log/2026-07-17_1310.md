## The question
How successful was the property-record name search (`karibe_matched.csv`) at
finding decree holders who own property? Use the reports panel as an independent
benchmark for homeownership.

## Data used (READ-ONLY from Dropbox `data/raw/`)
- `names_individuos22.csv` (3,181 Act 22 grantees; grantee, approval_date, first/last name)
- `names_individuos60.csv` (2,673 Act 60 grantees; same columns)
- `karibe_matched.csv` (11,464 property records: titular, demarcacion, localizacion,
  num_finca, desc_registral) = results returned when searching decree-holder names.
- Reports panel: `data/clean/act22_panel_full.dta` (8,446 person-years, 4,726 unique
  filers). Panel is a MIXED population: BOTH Act 22 and Act 60 filers.

## Key data facts / gotchas
- Annual reports are REDACTED (no names) -> cannot link report<->karibe person to
  person. All comparisons are AGGREGATE RATES.
- Panel has `re` (real estate wealth) but NO explicit Own/Rent; that field lives in
  the raw report files (`does_grantee_own_a_home..._rent_it`) + `primary_property_value`.
  Merge them onto the panel by filename (100% match achieved).
- HOMEOWNERSHIP is defined as owns a PRIMARY HOME (explicit "Own" OR primary value>0).
  `re>0` is NOT homeownership (can be investment/land) — do not use it for that.
- karibe has NO dates; the only per-person date is the decree `approval_date`.
- Stata gotcha (logged): building a local list from quoted items across `///`
  continuations strips quotes and concatenates -> `r(132) too few quotes`. Put the
  list on ONE line, unquoted.

## Headline numbers
- Reports homeownership (primary home, person level): ~58% (57.9% "Own"; 62.3% if
  including any real-estate, but that's not homeownership).
- Karibe search found-rate: Act 22 50.3%, Act 60 54.0%, all names 52.0%.
- False positives: random-name coincidence is small (~2.5%, placebo). Same-name
  (namesake) inflation is larger — found rate rises with surname commonness
  (unique surname 45%, 3+-share 62%); a namesake-robust discovery rate ~45%.

## FINAL INTERPRETATION (this is where we landed — important)
Do NOT frame this as strong "undercoverage." The reports homeownership rate and the
search found-rate are **broadly consistent / same ballpark (~45-60%)**. Where the two
series are genuinely comparable (2015-2017, mature Act 22, timing doesn't bind) they
essentially OVERLAP. They diverge only in recent years (2020-2022), which is explained
by CONFOUNDS — recent grantees have had less time to buy/register, and Act 60 floods
the denominator with brand-new people — not by the search getting worse over time.
Note the cohort chart's downward slope toward recent cohorts is an ACCUMULATION curve
(time-to-buy); a "search degrades on old data" story would predict the OPPOSITE slope,
and property registries reflect current title anyway. Bottom line: the name search is
a **credible proxy** for homeownership among decree holders; differences are within
what timing + composition + the namesake caveat would produce.
