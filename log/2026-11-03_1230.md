## 2. P(others follow): pioneers vs VIRGIN LUXURY controls
- `build_pioneer_follow.py` → `data/design1/pioneer_follow_units.csv`:
  237 pioneer events vs 11,367 island-wide virgin luxury purchases

  Outcomes: other-buyer decree events within 250/500/1000m in (0,k] years,
  k=1/2/3; obs_yk censoring flags (date+k ≤ 2025-12-31).
- `design1_pioneer_follow.do` → pioneer_follow_coefs.csv,
  pioneer_follow_results.log. LPM any-arrival on pioneer + year & municipio
  FE, cluster(muni); ppmlhdfe counts same FE.
- `plot_pioneer_follow.py` → fig_pioneer_follow.png/.pdf.
HEADLINE: within 3 years, another decree investor arrives within 500m of
25% of pioneer sites vs 16% of virgin luxury sites (raw); within 250m
16% vs 7%. Adjusted (yr+muni FE): 250m/3y +7.4pp (se 3.1, p=.021), PPML
+0.69 log pts (p=.003) ≈ doubling; 500m/3y +7.0pp (p=.077), PPML +0.56
(p=.023); 1km/3y +7.5pp (p=.051), PPML +0.38 (p=.057). Year-1/2 horizons
positive, ns. Proportional effect LARGEST at 250m → micro-clustering.
