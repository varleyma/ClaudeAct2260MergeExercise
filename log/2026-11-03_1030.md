# Log — 2026-11-03 10:30 — Buyer exposure + investor clustering descriptive (two-panel figure)

## Goal
Show investor spatial clustering DIRECTLY (user request), not just via the
density-split heterogeneity in the ring results: "odds an Act 60 investor is
already nearby when you move in."

## New scripts (code/python/)
- `build_exposure_clustering.py` — one streaming pass over the island CRIM
  snapshot (1,536,453 parcels) + design1 events (1,160 decree-era dated events
  2012–2025; luxury band [p25,p95] of investor amounts = [$280K, $3.95M]).
  * Market purchase = individual (non-corporate regex, same as placebo script)
    buyer, parcel NOT in the 1,874-parcel investor universe, SALESAMT ≥ $10K,
    valid date. 286,186 purchases 2012–2025.
  * Exposure = ≥1 design1 event with event_date STRICTLY BEFORE the sale date
    within 250/500/1000/2000 m (grid 0.022°, haversine).
  * Investor-side exposure excludes events by the same buyer (own prior
    purchases don't count as "another investor nearby").
  * IMPORTANT: placebo_events.csv could NOT be the clustering benchmark — those
    are co-located ≤250m from real events BY CONSTRUCTION (timing placebo).
    Benchmark = island-wide luxury-band individual purchases instead,
    reweighted to the investor events' year distribution for the odds ratios.
- `plot_cluster_exposure.py` — two-panel figure.

## Caveats (for figure notes / text)
- CRIM last-sale-only: early buyer cohorts thinner & selected toward parcels
  not resold since (within observed sales, exposure measured correctly).
- Investor universe = name-matched owners of record; LLC purchases invisible
  → exposure is a LOWER BOUND.
- Luxury benchmark shares amenity locations but not micro-location: the ×2 at
  250m shrinking to ×1.2 at 2km is exactly the fingerprint of micro-clustering.
